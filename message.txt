## 🔄 WORKFLOW SUPPORT

### Typical Visit Workflow:
1. ✅ Pet owner registers/checks in
2. ✅ Added to queue or appointment
3. ✅ Veterinarian conducts examination
4. ✅ Creates medical record with vital signs and diagnosis
5. ✅ Orders lab tests if needed
6. ✅ Prescribes medications
7. ✅ Schedules follow-up or surgery if required
8. ✅ Generate invoice
9. ✅ Process payment
10. ✅ Check out

### Boarding Workflow:
1. ✅ Schedule boarding appointment
2. ✅ Assign cage
3. ✅ Check-in (record time)
4. ✅ Set up feeding schedule
5. ✅ Add medication instructions if needed
6. ✅ Monitor during stay
7. ✅ Check-out (record time)
8. ✅ Generate invoice and collect payment

### Grooming Workflow:
1. ✅ Schedule grooming appointment
2. ✅ Select grooming services
3. ✅ Assign groomer
4. ✅ Record special instructions
5. ✅ Track grooming status
6. ✅ Complete service
7. ✅ Add to invoice
